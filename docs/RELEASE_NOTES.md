Keibord 1.0.8: a tighter suggestion bar.

- Less empty space above the keys: the suggestion bar is lower, and its words sit centred between the keyboard's top edge and the first key row (iOS adds its own padding above the bar, which made the words look too low).

Install `Keibord.ipa` with your signer (keep app groups for the app and the keyboard), SideStore with the Keibord source, or TestFlight (docs/INSTALL-NO-PC.md). Installing over an earlier version keeps your data.
