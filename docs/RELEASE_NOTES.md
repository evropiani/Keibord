Keibord 1.0.25: Apple's sticker icon.

- The sticker button in the emoji panel's bottom bar is now Apple's icon: the round sticker with its corner peeled over.
- Stickers still have to be pasted after tapping them in Keibord: iOS lets no other keyboard insert images. To insert one directly, hold 🌐, pick Emoji and tap the sticker in Apple's emoji keyboard; ABC brings you back to Keibord.

Install `Keibord.ipa` with your signer (keep app groups for the app and the keyboards), SideStore with the Keibord source, or TestFlight (docs/INSTALL-NO-PC.md). Installing over an earlier version keeps your data.
