Keibord 1.0.24: emoji the size of Apple's, holding 123, favourite emoji and stickers.

- Emoji panel: the emoji are as big as on Apple's keyboard, in four rows with Apple's spacing.
- Hold 123: the numbers and symbols show as soon as you touch it. While you hold it, tap symbols with another finger; when you let go, the letters come back. You can also slide from 123 to a symbol and let go. A normal tap still stays on the numbers.
- Favourite emoji: up to 8, first on the recent emoji page in the order you choose. Set them in the app under Settings › Emoji & Stickers › Favourite Emoji: type or tap emoji into the field, drag to sort, swipe to remove.
- Stickers: iOS lets no other keyboard read your iPhone's stickers or insert images, so Keibord keeps copies of its own. In the app, open Settings › Emoji & Stickers › Stickers, tap the field, switch to Apple's emoji keyboard, open your stickers and tap them one by one: each one is saved at once. In Keibord they are to the left of the recent emoji (swipe right, or the new sticker button in the bottom bar). Tapping one copies it; then tap the text field and choose Paste. Apps that take Apple's stickers, like Messages, should show it like one; other apps paste it as an image. Stickers with effects or animation are saved as still images. Needs Allow Full Access.

Install `Keibord.ipa` with your signer (keep app groups for the app and the keyboards), SideStore with the Keibord source, or TestFlight (docs/INSTALL-NO-PC.md). Installing over an earlier version keeps your data.
