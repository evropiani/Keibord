Keibord 1.0.23: the space bar like Apple's, Apple's emoji key, and emoji recents that stay.

- Space bar: each keyboard shows only its own language in the corner (DE on Keibord Deutsch, EN on Keibord English, …), no longer every language. Like Apple's keyboard, the language's name shows in the middle of the space bar for a moment when the keyboard appears and then fades out.
- The emoji key shows Apple's grinning face (outlined in light mode, filled in dark mode).
- Recently used emoji: they were only saved when the keyboard closed, and iOS often ends a keyboard without closing it (for example when you switch apps right after sending). They are now saved two seconds after you use one, when you leave the emoji panel, and whenever the app goes to the background. Emoji lost before this version cannot be brought back; from now on every one you use is kept.

Install `Keibord.ipa` with your signer (keep app groups for the app and the keyboards), SideStore with the Keibord source, or TestFlight (docs/INSTALL-NO-PC.md). Installing over an earlier version keeps your data.
