# Outside this list such a word is kept only as a Capitalised name the other variant's Hunspell
# knows ("Rebecca", "Liam"). The form gives the casing; the frequency comes from the corpus.
# A word the other variant's Hunspell knows ("outta" is en-US only) is kept in both through this list.
